Notifications are easy to miss. Calendar Alarm makes your phone ring a real alarm before the meetings that matter, showing the meeting's name, so you never miss one again.

HOW IT WORKS
• Connect your Google account. Calendar Alarm only reads your calendar; it never changes it.
• Tap the meetings you want an alarm for.
• Choose when it rings: 1, 5, 20 or 30 minutes before, any custom time, or several alarms for the same meeting (for example 45 and 5 minutes before).
• Your phone rings like an alarm clock: full screen over the lock screen, on the alarm volume, even when the phone is on silent. Snooze or dismiss with one tap.

ALARMS THAT FOLLOW YOUR CALENDAR
• Meeting moved? The alarm moves with it.
• Meeting cancelled? The alarm is removed.
• Offline? Your alarms stay exactly as they were.
• Alarms come back automatically after restarting your phone.

AUTOMATIC ALARMS (OPTIONAL)
Give every video call, or every meeting you accepted with other people, an alarm automatically. Turn one off and it stays off.

ALSO
• A default alarm time for all meetings, and per-meeting overrides
• Choose which calendars to show
• Home-screen widget with your next alarm
• Light and dark theme

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account with us, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Your calendar data stays on your phone and is only read from Google to show your events and ring your alarms.
