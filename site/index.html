Calendar Alarm

Never miss a meeting again. Pick the events in your Google Calendar that matter and your phone rings a real alarm — with the meeting's name — a few minutes before each one.

Download for Android Privacy policy

How it works

  1. Connect your Google account. The app only reads your calendar; it never changes it.
  2. Tap the meetings you want an alarm for.
  3. Choose when it rings: 1, 5, 20 or 30 minutes before, any custom time, or several alarms for the same meeting.
  4. Your phone rings like an alarm clock — full screen, even on silent — showing the meeting's name.

Features

  • Alarms follow your meetings: moved meetings move their alarm, cancelled meetings remove it.
  • Optional automatic alarms for video calls or meetings you accepted.
  • Snooze, a home-screen widget showing your next alarm, and alarms that survive restarts.
  • No account with us, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
