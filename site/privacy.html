Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Calendar Alarm ("the app") is an Android app that rings alarms before the Google Calendar meetings you choose. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: your data stays on your phone. The app has no servers and shares nothing with anyone.

Data the app accesses

  • Your Google account's email address, to show which account is connected.
  • Your Google Calendar events, read-only (scope calendar.readonly): the list of your calendars, and for upcoming events their title, start and end time, location, video-call link and whether you accepted the invitation. The app never creates, changes or deletes anything in your calendar.

How the data is used

Only to show your upcoming events in the app and to ring alarms before the ones you select (including the optional rules that select video calls or accepted meetings automatically). Calendar data is fetched directly from Google by the app on your phone.

What is stored, and where

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • the events you selected (event id, title, start time and the alarm times you chose),
  • your settings (default alarm time, chosen calendars, automatic-alarm rules, events you turned off),
  • the email of the connected Google account.

Full event lists are not stored; they are loaded from Google when you open the app or when the app checks for changes to your selected meetings.

Sharing

None. The app has no backend servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. Your data is not sold, transferred or shared with anyone. The only network connections the app makes are to Google (sign-in and the Google Calendar API).

Google API Services

Calendar Alarm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Disconnect removes the account from the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
